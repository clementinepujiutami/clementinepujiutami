## What I do ✦

- **Design:** interfaces, design systems, and the small spacing details people feel but never notice.
- **Build:** fast, tidy sites and apps in Next.js, WordPress and Sanity.
- **Get found:** SEO, AEO and GEO, so search engines and AI answers both understand the site.
- **Automate:** AI helpers and workflows that take the boring parts off your plate.
